## Design Note

Orbital Retro-Future is an original Pixelody design. Classic science-fiction game interfaces were used only as high-level visual reference. No Marathon logos, screenshots, textures, character art, or other copyrighted game assets are bundled.

Bulkhead Terminal is an unofficial original homage and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bungie. Official Aleph One preservation screenshots were consulted as visual reference only; no files from those screenshots or game data are distributed with Pixelody.

Graphite Loadout is a separate unofficial original homage based specifically on the visual direction shown on Bungie's current official Marathon website: https://www.marathonthegame.com/. Official screenshots and user-provided gameplay references were consulted but are not bundled. Its grids, panels, and animation geometry are authored in CSS and do not reproduce Bungie logos, characters, weapons, environments, or UI artwork.

Cosmic Cinema uses commercial film posters supplied by the user only as high-level mood references. No poster artwork, actor likenesses, title treatments, layouts, or social-media images are bundled. Its event horizons, orbital rings, grain, glow, and archive details are original CSS and SVG geometry. `signal-relic.svg` and `calibration-halo.svg` are original Pixelody assets and require no third-party attribution.

Frosted Void uses supplied commercial imagery only as high-level material, curvature, and lighting reference. No stock bubble image, face, hat, portrait silhouette, title treatment, layout, or poster artwork is bundled. `audio-monolith.svg` and `spectral-bubbles.svg` are original Pixelody assets and require no third-party attribution.

Obsession Mode is an original Pixelody psychological-thriller poster theme. Commercial thriller posters, film stills, character art, faces, masks, gore, logos, and copyrighted title treatments were not bundled. `src/assets/themes/obsession/fracture-overlay.svg` is original Pixelody abstract geometry and requires no third-party attribution.
